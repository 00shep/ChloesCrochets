About Me

Hi, I'm Chloe!

I taught myself to crochet and I love turning a ball of yarn into something people are excited to take home. Every item on this site is made by me, one stitch at a time — that's part of why each one takes a little while and why no two are ever exactly the same.

I sell my crochet at local fairs and markets, and I'm building this site so you can see my work, catch me at my next event, or place an order any time in between.

How It Works

Made-to-order, made for you

Most pieces can be customized. Here's what to expect when you order.

Step 1

Pick your piece

Choose a design, then pick a size and color if the piece offers options.

Step 2

I get crocheting

Everything is made to order by hand, so it takes some time — typically 2–3 weeks depending on the piece.

Step 3

Pickup, ship, or fair

Grab it at a local fair, arrange local pickup, or have it shipped right to you.
